# AutoLeadForce — Legal & Tax Guide (India)

To move from "Freelancer" to "Agency," follow these zero-cost legal steps to establish your identity.

---

## 1. Udyam Registration (MSME Certificate)
This is your official "Business License" from the Govt of India. It is free and takes 10 minutes.

- **Site**: [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in/)
- **Registration Type**: "For New Entrepreneurs who are not Registered yet as MSME."
- **Details Needed**: 
    - **Aadhaar Number** (linked to mobile for OTP).
    - **PAN Number**.
    - **Enterprise Name**: AutoLeadForce
    - **NIC Code**: 62011 (Software publishing, consultancy and related activities).
    - **Address**: Your primary work location (Home is fine).

---

## 2. Tax & GST Structure
Since we are following the Zero-Cost model, we optimize for minimum compliance.

1.  **GST Threshold**: You do **NOT** need to register for GST until your annual turnover (revenue) exceeds **₹20 Lakhs**.
    *   *Decision*: Stay unregistered for now to avoid the monthly filing headache.
2.  **PAN Card**: As a Sole Proprietorship, the agency's PAN is **your personal PAN**. 
    *   *Action*: Use your personal PAN for Razorpay, Zoho, and Bank accounts.
3.  **Invoicing**: Mention your PAN on the invoices generated by Zoho for transparency.

---

## 3. Banking Protocol
To track the **Autonomous Revenue Engine**, keep the money flow clean.

- **Option A (Professional)**: Take your Udyam Certificate to a bank (HDFC/ICICI/Kotak) and open a **Current Account** in the name of **AutoLeadForce**
- **Option B (Zero-Cost)**: Create a separate **Savings Account** (or a dedicated "Folder" in certain mobile banks) to only receive client payments.
