# The Razor Pitch Protocol: Free Website Preview

> **Goal**: 100% conversion from Interest to "Send me the link."
> **Psychology**: Frictionless entry. I did the work first; you just have to look at it.

---

## 1. The "Cold Pivot" (WhatsApp / SMS)

**Subject**: (No subject, direct message)

"Hi [Owner Name], this is Nabab from **AutoLeadForce**

I was looking at [Business Name]'s online presence and noticed your current site is [slow / not mobile-friendly / non-existent]. 

Instead of a sales pitch, I just went ahead and built a **premium preview** of how your business *should* look in 2026. 

It has:
- ✅ **Lightning Fast** load speeds
- ✅ **WhatsApp Integration** (Direct leads)
- ✅ **Premium Brutalist Design**

It's live at a private link right now. **Should I send you the link to check it out?** (Zero cost, no string attached)."

---

## 2. The "Bridge" (Once they say yes)

"Great! Here it is: [Vercel-Link]

Open it on your phone — that's where 80% of your customers are. 

If you like the design and want to move your business to this speed/look, we can handle the domain setup and maintenance for you. If not, no worries — the preview is yours to keep for reference."

---

## 3. The "Blade" Close (After they see it)

"The site is ready to go live on [businessname.com]. 

We handle:
1. **Domain & SSL** (Security)
2. **High-Speed Hosting**
3. **Monthly Tech Maintenance**
4. **WhatsApp Lead Tracking**

Total to go live: ₹[X],000 (One-time setup) + ₹[Y],000 (Monthly scale plan). 

Should we wire up the domain today?"

---

## 4. The "AI Problem Solver" (For clients WITH a website)

**Goal**: Target the gap in their current system (Missed calls / Slow response).

**Message**:
"Hi [Owner Name], this is Nabab from **AutoLeadForce**

I checked out your website — it looks good! However, I noticed a major **Revenue Gap** in your setup: your business is likely losing 30-40% of leads because you don't have an **Autonomous Response Engine**.

I've mapped out a quick **AI Automation Blueprint** for [Business Name] that:
- 🤖 Automatically handles missed calls via WhatsApp.
- 🚀 Instant-responds to GMB inquiries 24/7.
- 📈 Captures leads without you touching your phone.

I've already drafted the logic for your business. **Can I send you a 1-minute breakdown of how this plugs into your current site?**"

---

"Since you already have a website you like, we don't need to change that. 

We can simply plug in our **Growth Blade (₹3,500/mo)**. It sits on top of your current site and handles all the 'Revenue Boring' work:
1. **Missed-Call WhatsApp Bot**
