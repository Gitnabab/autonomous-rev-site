# AutoLeadForce — Financial Setup Guide

To automate your revenue, please complete these two setups. 

---

## 1. Zoho Invoice (The Billing Engine)
Used for professional, automated invoicing and tracking payments. **Free Forever.**

1.  **Register**: Go to [Zoho Invoice Signup](https://www.zoho.com/in/invoice/signup/).
2.  **Organization Info**:
    *   **Org Name**: AutoLeadForce
    *   **Currency**: INR
3.  **Branding**:
    *   Upload your logo (if available).
    *   Set your primary color as **Blue** or **Black**.
4.  **Recurring Invoices**: This is key for the "Blade" model. We will set these up once you have your first client.

---

## 2. Razorpay (The Payment Gateway)
Used to collect UPI, Cards, and Netbanking payments instantly.

1.  **Register**: Go to [Razorpay Signup](https://razorpay.com/signup/).
2.  **Account Type**: Select "Individual" (unless you have a registered company PAN already).
3.  **KYC Required**: You will need to provide:
    *   PAN Card
    *   Business Details (Use "Professional Services" or "Software as a Service")
4.  **Payment Links**: Once active, we can generate instant links to send on WhatsApp.
