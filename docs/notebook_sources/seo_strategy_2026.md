# SEO & Content Strategy for the Generative Era (2026)

Traditional SEO is dead. Content must now be optimized for **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** and **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)**.

## 1. Optimization for AI Citations
*   **GEO Focus**: Making content extractable and citable by AI Overviews and SGE.
*   **Direct Answers**: Start every section with a factual, direct answer to a likely user query.
*   **Data & Statistics**: AI models prioritize grounded facts. Cite original research and specific data.

## 2. Authority & Trust (E-E-A-T)
*   **Experience**: Show real-world evidence.
*   **Expertise**: Verifiable credentials and research-backed data.
*   **Trust**: High-quality, non-repetitive writing with clear citations.

## 3. Structural Standards for AI
*   **Hierarchical Headings**: Logical H1/H2/H3 flows.
*   **Scannability**: Heavy use of bullet points, tables, and short summaries.
*   **Schema Markup**: Implement FAQPage and HowTo schema for better AI indexing.

## 4. Entity-Based SEO
*   AI understands "Entities" (concepts/relationships) over keywords.
*   Topics must be covered with deep entity-rich authority across the entire subject.
