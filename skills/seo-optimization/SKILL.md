## Operational Rules

### Must Do
- Always prioritize GBP optimization FIRST — it's free and most impactful
- Use the title tag formula: `[Service] in [City] — [Business Name] | [Value Prop]`
- Include JSON-LD LocalBusiness schema on every client website
- Track NAP consistency across all directories
- Set up Google Search Console and submit sitemap
- Target location-specific long-tail keywords

### Never Do
- Keyword stuff business names on GBP (Google penalizes this)
- Buy fake reviews (instant ban risk)
- Ignore mobile optimization (Google uses mobile-first indexing)
- Focus on vanity metrics (rankings alone don't matter, leads do)
- Skip local citations — they're free and powerful
- Overlook voice search optimization
